## AI-050 Meshy production brief v1

Create a stylized 3D battlefield character named Skyline Seer for a mythic Greek lightning-and-sky faction in a turn-based tactical board game. The figure is an original adult oracle-warrior, not a depiction of an existing copyrighted character. Prioritize a strong readable silhouette from an elevated three-quarter camera: layered white and deep-blue robes, restrained bronze armor, a forked lightning staff, wind-swept mantle, and subtle storm motifs. The character should feel wise, mobile, and dangerous rather than bulky. Use clean game-ready geometry, separated logical materials, symmetrical neutral A-pose or relaxed combat-ready stance suitable for later rigging, closed watertight surfaces where practical, no base fused to the feet, no text, no logos, no gore, no photoreal human skin, no extreme cloth wisps, and no tiny floating pieces. Target Unity-friendly GLB, upright Y axis, forward +Z, feet at origin, approximately 1.8 Unity units tall after import. Provide a preview and the generated model; do not invent gameplay mechanics or ask follow-up questions.

## AI-051 ElevenLabs sound brief v1

Create short, nonverbal game sound effects for Skyline Seer, a mythic Greek storm oracle in a turn-based tactical board game. The shared palette is airy electricity, bronze resonance, controlled wind, and faint mystical chimes. Avoid speech, chanting, melody, thunderclap cliches, modern machinery, gun-like transients, excessive bass, and long reverb tails. Cues: deploy (0.7 s, descending air rush ending in a precise electric seal); move (0.35 s, light cloth/wind step with a tiny static lift); ranged attack (0.8 s, focused staff charge and sharp sky-bolt release); hit (0.3 s, brief electrical crack with restrained bronze impact); destroy (1.1 s, collapsing storm energy, cloth fall, and fading chime). Produce discrete files, not a montage. Integration target is 48 kHz WAV; final loudness and peak normalization happen during game integration.
