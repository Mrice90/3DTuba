# Rig fixes after the laptop import check (2026-10-18)

The laptop build `playtest\unity-build-rig-combat-2026-10-18` flagged four problems. 16 characters were re-rigged in cloud Blender 5.2.2 (no Meshy credits). Fixed FBXs are in the project files folder `rig-combat-2026-10-18-fixes/models/` (files over 12,000,000 bytes also split into 10,000,000-byte parts in `models-split/`, checksums in both folders).

## What was wrong and what changed

| Finding | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Thunder Ram scrambled with its in-game texture | Rigged from the batch-04b Meshy model; the game uses the textured AI-052 model (8,015 verts) | New skeleton (`cfg/zeus_siege_thunder_ram.json`, 4 legs, 4-bone neck/head, 4-bone tail) on `assets/staging/meshy/AI-052-zeus_siege_thunder_ram/textured/zeus_siege_thunder_ram_textured.glb` |
| Aegis Airguard, Arc Relay Scout, Hera, Keraunos Prime, Stormgate Sentinel, Tempest Oracle "different model version"; Iris, Sparkstep, Cloudline Raider slimmer | Not a different model: every one of these rest meshes is vertex-for-vertex identical to the in-game token (`scripts/cmprest.py`, nearest-vertex distance 0 against `UnityProof/Assets/RigCompare/<card>.fbx`). Six of them had `arm_down` 18-22 deg, which lowered the arms in every clip, so the idle silhouette was narrower than the card art (width ratio 0.67-0.88 in the laptop check) | `arm_down` removed; all nine re-rigged from the in-game token meshes. Idle frame 1 now sits within 0.3% of model height (mean vertex offset) of the token pose |
| Reef Tunneler and Keraunos Seraph jump up at the start of Attack | Hover units were grounded with one constant offset per clip, so a clip whose lowest frame was mid-strike started above the tile (19.5% and 12.4% of height) | `rig_posed.py`: hover clips are now grounded every frame like walkers, and their float is added on top as a lift that is 0 on frame 1 (`clips.py` hover bobs use `1 - cos`). Reef Tunneler also gets `attack_pitch 0.3` so its long body strikes forward instead of diving its head. Naiad Flowshaper (Walk 4%) and Eagle of the High Grid (Attack 4%) had the same issue and were re-rigged too |
| Tidepool Surveyor and Undertow Stalker end Death standing upright | `death_roll` 100/80 deg rolled the flat bodies onto their edge | `death_roll 12` and new `death_splay` (70/60 deg): legs splay out, the body drops flat onto the tile |

## Checks (cloud, all 16)

- Every clip of every model: lowest vertex within 0.0001 H of the tile on every frame (`scripts/actz.py`, same numbers as Unity's sampling); every clip's first frame on the tile (`scripts/idlecmp.py`).
- Six takes kept (Idle, Walk, Attack, Hit, Death, Summon), same frame counts and event frames as before.
- Worst stretch (share of edges stretched past 2.5x on any frame): 1.46% (Tempest Oracle Death), all under 1.5%.
- Review sheets and GIFs: `review/<card>_sheet.png` here, GIFs in the project files folder.
