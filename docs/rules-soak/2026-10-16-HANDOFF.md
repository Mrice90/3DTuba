# Rules engine soak: 0.4.5 hex37 core, handoff for ChatGPT

Claude Code, rules soak track, 2026-10-16. Test-and-report only: nothing in the engine was changed. Every finding below is a repro for the engine owner to rule on or fix.

## What was tested

- **Core:** the shipped 0.4.5 core. `InfiniteConquest.Native.dll` is `7f72db84…` and `InfiniteConquest.RulesCore.dll` is `cb527524…`, with `rules_version` `purchase-v3-hex37` on the 37-hex board (radius 3, capitals at (3,0) and (3,6)). DLL and data hashes match `playtest/unity-build-release-candidate-0.4.5/SHA256SUMS.txt`. A pinned copy is at `tools/rules-soak/engine/0.4.5/` with its own `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- **Harness:** `tools/rules-soak` (branch `claude/rules-soak-olciz6`). It runs headless on .NET 8 on Linux and calls `NativeRulesSession.Handle` exactly as Unity and the referee do.
- **Volume:** 7,600 matches and 1,577,693 actions. Every Zeus/Poseidon pairing was played, mirrors included, with both factions in both seats. A second full pass of 1,600 matches re-ran in fresh processes to check determinism.
  - 3,600 multiplayer-protocol matches (`op:new multiplayer:true`, one intent per action id). This is the referee path. Agents were aggro vs aggro, aggro vs random and random vs aggro.
  - 4,000 bot-vs-bot matches. The shipping `PlaytestBot` played both seats at MORTAL, HERO and DEMIGOD. Each bot transcript was then replayed through the multiplayer protocol, and the hash had to match at every step.
- **Checks after every action:**
  - No crash, `INTERNAL` error, hang, stall or empty legal set.
  - Every offered action is accepted.
  - No duplicate or unregistered instance, and each card's zone matches the list it sits in. Nothing goes off-board, and positions stay consistent with stacks.
  - GP is never negative. The match keeps exactly one Capital per side while live, and has a winner if and only if it is GAME_OVER.
  - Cards are conserved. No live permanent sits on the top of its stack at lethal damage, and no Capital is at 0 HP while the match is live.
  - Slots used never exceed slots total.
  - `op:hash` is stable.
  - Same seed plus same intents gives the same per-step hash, both in-process and across processes.

## F1: Capital stays alive at 0 HP while covered (needs a ruling or fix)

**Repro:** `tools/rules-soak/cases/0.4.5/known-findings/F1-capital-at-zero-hp-while-covered.json` (seed 100, Poseidon seat 0 vs Zeus seat 1, 109 intents). Run it with:

```
cd tools/rules-soak && dotnet build -c Release -o out && out/RulesSoak trace --data engine/0.4.5/data --case cases/0.4.5/known-findings/F1-capital-at-zero-hp-while-covered.json --from 100
```

**What happens:**
1. A Kraken Tendril Drone stands on top of the Poseidon Capital at (3,0).
2. Zeus activates Storm Relay Pylon (`DAMAGE_ENEMY_CAPITAL`) and other capital-damage structures, turn after turn.
3. Line of sight is satisfied, so the ability resolves. The Capital reaches 20/20 damage, and in other seeds it goes past 20.
4. The match stays in PLAY. The snapshot reports `hp: 0` for a live Capital.
5. In the October 11 repro, the covering unit later walks off. The bare Capital, at 20/20, is still not destroyed.

**Cause:** `CardAbilityRules.Resolve`, case `DAMAGE_ENEMY_CAPITAL`, adds the damage but calls `state.Destroy` only when the Capital is the top of its stack. `GameState.Destroy` re-checks the newly exposed top card only when the cover is destroyed, not when it moves away. The exhaustion-damage path in `GameState.DrawCard` uses the same top-only check, but it is unused under the purchase economy.

**Rate:** 43 of 7,600 matches (1 in 177).

**Rule question:** should damage to a covered Capital (a) be blocked, (b) be capped at HP−1 while covered, or (c) count, with the Capital destroyed as soon as damage reaches HP? Whichever you choose, the "uncovered at ≥ HP and still alive" state probably needs a re-check after moves and blinks. The UI currently shows a 0 HP Capital in a live match.

## F2: Unit stays on board with no slot supply (rule question)

**Repro:** `cases/0.4.5/known-findings/F2-slot-overflow-after-supply-structure-destroyed.json` (seed 20, 32 intents).

**What happens:**
1. Poseidon's only supply structure, Moonwell Tidegate (supply 2), is destroyed by a Zeus attack at intent 31.
2. Seat 1 now reports `slots_used 1 / slots_total 0`.
3. The character stays on the board.

**Rate:** 66 of 7,600 matches.

This may be intended (units already deployed stay), so it is a ruling, not necessarily a bug. If it is intended, I'll drop the invariant.

## F3: Poseidon mirror can stall past turn 300 (bot and balance)

**What happens:** all 31 no-result matches are Poseidon vs Poseidon: 16 HERO, 10 DEMIGOD, 2 MORTAL and 3 aggro scripts. Healing and defensive structures out-pace damage, and the turn counter passes 300.

**Why it matters for launch:**
- In solo play, `NativeRulesSession.RunBots` throws `"match did not finish within limits"` past turn 300, and the client receives an `INTERNAL` error.
- Online play has no turn limit at all.

**Suggestion:** decide on a turn or time limit, or a tie-break rule, for every mode.

## Observations, no action required

- **Balance:** in bot-vs-bot games, Zeus wins 54% against Poseidon (1,085 of 1,999). Seat 1 wins 53% overall.
- **DEMIGOD seat skew:** DEMIGOD vs DEMIGOD is lopsided by seat. Seat 1 wins 718 and seat 0 wins 471 (60%). HERO is even at 798 vs 786. Worth checking for a seat-dependent heuristic in `PlaytestBot.DemigodChoice`/`Evaluate` (`PlaytestBot.BotId = 1` is still a constant).
- **Referee notes:**
  - `op:hash` leaves out `reaction_pending` and `revision`.
  - `op:new` without `seed` falls back to the wall clock.

## Regression suite

`tools/rules-soak/test.sh` checks the pinned engine hashes and replays every case in `cases/0.4.5/`:
- `clean/` holds 10 full games (all matchups, both seats, bot and scripted). The state hash after every intent must reproduce exactly.
- `known-findings/` holds F1 and F2. They pass while the finding still reproduces. When a fix lands, they fail on purpose: re-record them and move them to `clean/`.

For a new engine drop, put it in `engine/<version>/` and run `./soak.sh engine/<version> 1 100` for a fresh soak plus a cross-process determinism check.
